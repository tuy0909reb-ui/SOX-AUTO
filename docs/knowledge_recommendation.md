---

## 1. AI が可能

* Candidate Generation
* Duplicate Detection
* Related Knowledge
* Recommendation
* Summarization
* Classification

---

## 2. AI が不可

* Approval
* Policy Change
* Production Change

AI Recommendation は Decision Gate / Human Approval の入力であり、最終判断を代替しない。
