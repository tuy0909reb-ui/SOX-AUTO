仕様: `docs/specs/operational_knowledge_evolution_phase11_4.md`  
親文書: `docs/operational_knowledge_evolution.md`

Human / AI / Automation Boundary を定義する。

---

## 1. Human

* Approval
* Validation
* Publication

---

## 2. AI

* Summarization
* Recommendation
* Classification
* Duplicate Detection

---

## 3. Automation

* Collection
* Indexing
* Notification

---

## 4. 禁止

* Knowledge による Production 自動変更
* AI による Approval / Policy Change / Production Change
* Human Approval 省略
* Research / Draft / Experimental Knowledge の正式 Publish
