# 1. 目的



Phase10-4 Future Operational Optimization は、



* Phase10-0 Production Adoption Governance  

* Phase10-1 Controlled Production Adoption  

* Phase10-2 Operational Validation  

* Phase10-3 Operational Feedback Integration  



を前提として、



**Production運用で蓄積された Validation結果・Feedback・Knowledge・改善履歴を基に、将来の運用品質向上を計画するための統制方式を定義するフェーズ**である。



本フェーズでは以下を扱う。



* Operational Optimization  

* Continuous Improvement Strategy  

* Efficiency Improvement  

* Reliability Improvement  

* Knowledge Optimization  

* Process Optimization  

* AI Assisted Optimization  

* Automation Improvement Research  

* Optimization Governance  

* Future Capability Planning  

* Optimization Prioritization  

* Optimization Decision Gate  

* Optimization Knowledge Feedback

# 7. AI Assisted Optimization Boundary



AIが可能：



* Trend Analysis  

* Pattern Detection  

* Optimization Recommendation  

* Simulation  



AIが不可：



* Optimization Approval  

* Production Change  

* Policy Change  

* Autonomous Improvement
