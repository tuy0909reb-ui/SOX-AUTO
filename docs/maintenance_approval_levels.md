## 1. Levels

```text
Minor Maintenance
    ↓
Owner Approval

Standard Maintenance
    ↓
Owner Approval
    ↓
Operational Governance Review

Critical Maintenance
    ↓
Governance Review
    ↓
Owner Approval
```

Human Approval 必須。AI による Approval は禁止。
