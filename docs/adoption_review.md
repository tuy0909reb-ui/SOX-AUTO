# Adoption Review（Phase10-0）

仕様: `docs/specs/production_adoption_governance_phase10_0.md`  
親文書: `docs/production_adoption_governance.md`

Adoption Review Process と判定結果を定義する。最終承認は Human。

---

## 1. Review Process

```text
Research Recommendation
        ↓
Technical Review
        ↓
Operational Review
        ↓
Final Human Approval
```

---

## 2. Review Criteria（Adoption Criteria）

* Technical Validation
* Operational Validation
* Governance Compliance
* Security Review
* Reliability Review
* Risk Assessment
* Knowledge Availability
* Documentation Completeness

---

## 3. Review 対象

* 技術妥当性
* 運用妥当性
* AI妥当性
* Policy整合性
* Security
* Reliability
* Explainability

---

## 4. Review Result

* Approved
* Deferred
* Rejected

原則:

```text
AI may recommend.
AI never approves.
Human is the final authority.
```
