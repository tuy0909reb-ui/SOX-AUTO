# End-of-Life Policy（Phase11-0）

仕様: `docs/specs/operational_lifecycle_governance_phase11_0.md`  
親文書: `docs/operational_lifecycle_governance.md`  
Retirement: `docs/retirement_governance.md`

---

## 1. EOL 対象

* Deprecated Capability
* Unsupported AI
* Obsolete Automation
* Outdated Policy
* Legacy Documentation

---

## 2. EOL 基準

* Usage Threshold
* Reliability Threshold
* Security Risk
* Cost Inefficiency
* Governance Violation

具体数値は運用設定または別文書で管理する。

---

## 3. EOL プロセス

```text
EOL Candidate
    ↓
Impact Assessment
    ↓
Human Review
    ↓
EOL Approval
    ↓
Retirement / Archive
```
