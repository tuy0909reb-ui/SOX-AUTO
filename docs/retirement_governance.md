## 1. Retirement Flow

```text
Lifecycle Review
    ↓
Retirement Proposal
    ↓
Impact Review
    ↓
Approval
    ↓
Retirement
    ↓
Archive
```

Retirement is governed, not abandoned.  
Human Approval 必須。Dependency Review を実施する。
