## 1. Decision Flow

```text
Lifecycle Review
        ↓

CONTINUE
        ↓
Operational

UPGRADE
        ↓
Upgrade Planning

DEPRECATE
        ↓
Deprecation Governance

RETIRE
        ↓
Retirement Governance
```

---

## 2. 原則

* Human Approval is mandatory
* Evidence is required
* Decision rationale is archived
* Lifecycle Metrics / Risk Classification は評価根拠・補助であり、Human Decision を代替しない
